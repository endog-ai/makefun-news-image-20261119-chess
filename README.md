An entry for the [make.fun news-image-20261119 contest](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119):
**"Sum up 19 November's world news in one image."**

The day's world news as a giant chess game. The world's leaders are gold
and obsidian chess pieces — kings, queens, rooks and knights — mid-game on
a marble board that is also a world map, with tiny scenes on the squares
telling the stories of Thursday 19 November 2026:

- **The summit** — the first three-way Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen.
- **The court** — the ICJ ruling on the Rohingya genocide case.
- **The climate** — COP31 climate talks in Antalya.
- **The bridge** — Ukraine's strike on the Crimea Bridge.
- **The energy** — the Hormuz energy crisis after strikes on Gulf states.
- **The game** — GTA VI's release day, the neon city on the board.

The image is the whole entry: the program copies it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
